01 — The brief

Player selection, backed by numbers.

T20 squad selection is often argued on reputation and recent form. I built a dashboard that scores selections against a data-driven win-probability model, so the argument has numbers behind it.

02 — The problem

Where gut-feel selection breaks down.

Bias

Recency bias skews picks

A player's last few innings tend to dominate selection conversations more than their full-season performance profile.

Context

Match conditions get ignored

Pitch, opposition, and venue all shift which player profile actually improves win probability — hard to weigh by eye.

Comparison

No single view to compare options

Stats are scattered across scorecards and summaries, with no single dashboard to compare candidate XIs side by side.

05 — Under the hood

The engineering decisions.

Power BIPythonPandasData Analysis
Pipeline

Python feeds Power BI.

Pandas handles the cleaning and feature work upstream; Power BI is used purely for the interactive, explorable layer on top.

Modeling

A score, not just a stat sheet.

Player data is combined into a single win-probability score per selection, rather than leaving the viewer to weigh raw stats themselves.

06 — Honest scope

What this dashboard doesn't do yet.

Historical data only

The model scores past tournament data; it isn't wired to a live feed for in-progress matches.

No opposition-specific tuning

The win-probability score doesn't yet weight matchup-specific factors like a batter's record against a particular bowling attack.

Next steps

Where this goes next.

Live data

Wire in a live match feed so selections can be scored during a tournament, not just after it.

Matchup modeling

Add opponent-specific weighting to the win-probability score.
